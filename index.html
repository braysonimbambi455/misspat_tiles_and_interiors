Company Overview

Built on Quality, Driven by Style

Miss Pat Tiles & Interiors Ltd is a distinguished provider of premium tiling and interior design solutions. With a commitment to excellence and focus on delivering top-tier services, we have emerged as a trusted name in the interior design sector. Our mission is to provide comprehensive interior solutions that exceed our clients' expectations, ensuring optimal value while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and integrity.

The company offers a diverse range of services tailored to meet the unique needs of homeowners, businesses, and developers. We provide valuable advice to clients on design choices and materials, guiding them on strategies to maximise the aesthetic and functional value of their spaces.

Our vision is to be the leading provider of interior design and tiling solutions in Kenya, recognised for our unwavering commitment to excellence, style, and client satisfaction.

With our dedicated team of professionals and a customer-centric approach, we continue to set new standards of excellence in interior design. We are committed to delivering unparalleled services that add value to our clients' spaces and contribute to the growth and prosperity of the interior design industry in Kenya.
